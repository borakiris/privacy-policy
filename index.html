1. Who we are

LeadOrbit (the “Service”) is a lead management application for sales teams. It is provided by Lucithinx, located at 5186 Sokak No: 9/2, İzmir, Türkiye (“we”, “us”, “our”).

The Service has three parts:

  • a web application, where organizations manage campaigns, leads, messages and replies;
  • a Chrome browser extension that works on LinkedIn pages in the user's own browser;
  • a backend that stores the data and runs AI and email features.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy explains how we process personal data about:

  • Users: the people who sign in to the Service on behalf of an organization (our customer);
  • Prospects: the people whose professional profile information users add to their organization's lead lists.

For prospect data, the customer organization decides which people to collect and contact, and why. In most cases the customer is therefore the controller of that data and we act as its processor. For account and usage data about our users, we are the controller. Customers can ask us for a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) at the contact address in section 19.

3. Relationship with LinkedIn

The Service is an independent product. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or partnered with LinkedIn Corporation. “LinkedIn” is a trademark of LinkedIn Corporation and is used here only to describe the website the extension works with.

Users are responsible for using LinkedIn in line with LinkedIn's own User Agreement and policies. Some automated activity on LinkedIn may be restricted by those terms.

4. Data we collect

4.1 Account and organization data (users)

  • Name, email address and password for signing in. Passwords are managed by our authentication provider and are stored only in hashed form.
  • Organization name, membership and role (for example admin or member), and license status.
  • Registered browser extension installations: a hashed installation identifier, a label, activation date, last-seen time and revocation status.
  • Sending settings for a connected LinkedIn sender:
    • display name;
    • daily send limit and minimum interval between messages;
    • whether automatic sending, automatic start and reply checking are turned on;
    • time of last send and the last error.

4.2 Lead (prospect) data

When a user chooses to import results from a LinkedIn or Sales Navigator search page, the extension reads the information shown on that page and sends it to the user's organization in the Service:

  • profile URL and profile type (public profile or Sales Navigator lead);
  • first, last and full name;
  • job title, company, location and headline, if shown;
  • connection degree (for example 1st, 2nd or 3rd+);
  • source, import batch, and collection and update times.

We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, private messages unrelated to a campaign, connection lists, or any information that is not shown on the page the user imports from.

4.3 Campaign and outreach data

  • Campaign settings, such as target titles, industries, company sizes, locations and message templates.
  • The leads included in each campaign and whether they are selected.
  • Outreach status, queue state, send attempts, timestamps and error codes.
  • The text of outreach messages created or approved by users.

4.4 Reply and notification data

  • If a user turns on reply checking, the extension reads the LinkedIn conversations with leads in the user's campaigns. It stores the message text, its direction (sent or received), its time and a message identifier.
  • The AI classification of each reply (for example interested, not interested or out of office) and its reasoning.
  • Email notifications sent to users about replies: recipient, subject, content and delivery status.

4.5 Technical data

  • Server logs: request times, error details and the identifiers needed to operate and secure the Service.
  • Diagnostic page-structure snapshots, only if a user chooses to copy one and send it to support.

We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics in the web application or the extension. If this changes, we will update this policy first.

4.6 What we never collect

  • LinkedIn passwords.
  • LinkedIn session cookies or login tokens. The extension relies on the user's existing LinkedIn session in their own browser and never sends it to us.
  • Payment card details. The Service does not process payments.

5. How we use data

  • To provide the Service: store leads, organize campaigns, prepare and queue messages, and show replies.
  • To send LinkedIn messages from the user's own account, as the user has configured (see section 7).
  • To score leads against a campaign's target profile and to help draft messages (see section 6).
  • To detect replies, classify them and notify the right users by email.
  • To enforce the sending limits and pacing that users set, and to prevent duplicate messages.
  • To secure the Service: authenticate users and devices, keep organizations' data separate, and prevent abuse.
  • To provide support and to communicate important changes to the Service.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

6. AI lead scoring and message assistance

The Service uses a third-party AI model provider (see section 9) for three tasks:

  • Lead scoring. A lead's name, job title, company, location and headline are compared with the campaign's target criteria. The result is a score, a match level and a short reason.
  • Message assistance. The model may lightly adapt a message template written by the user, using only the lead's profile fields. It is instructed not to invent facts about the person.
  • Reply classification. The text of a lead's reply, together with the lead's name, title and company, is classified (for example interested or out of office) so that users can prioritize follow-ups.

AI results support the user's decisions. They do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Users review leads and approve messages, or switch automatic sending on themselves. AI output can be wrong and should be checked.

We use these providers through their paid API services. Under the providers’ terms for these services, the data we send is not used to train their models. The providers may keep it for a limited time for abuse monitoring, as described in their terms.

7. LinkedIn-related processing

The browser extension runs only on https://www.linkedin.com/*, inside the user's own browser, and only while the user is signed in to LinkedIn. It:

  • reads lead information from search results when the user starts an import;
  • opens lead profiles or messaging pages to send campaign messages. Depending on the user's settings, the user either reviews and sends each message, or allows automatic sending within the limits they set;
  • reads conversations with campaign leads to detect replies, only if the user turns on reply checking;
  • saves its working state locally in the browser (see section 16).

The extension does not bypass CAPTCHAs or security checks, does not disguise automated activity, and does not use non-public LinkedIn interfaces. If LinkedIn asks the user to sign in or verify their account, outreach stops until the user deals with it.

11. Data security

  • Organization isolation: database row-level security keeps each organization's data separate. Organization membership is checked on the server and is never taken from the browser.
  • Encryption in transit: all traffic to the Service uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Hosting: data and backups are stored encrypted at rest by our hosting provider.
  • Device registration: each browser extension must be activated for a user and can be revoked.
  • Minimal credentials: no LinkedIn passwords or session cookies are sent to or stored by us. AI and email API keys are kept only on the server.
  • Safe sending: server-side limits, pacing and safeguards against sending the same message twice.
  • Access: only authorized Lucithinx personnel can access production systems. Access is protected by strong authentication and used only to operate, support and secure the Service.

No system is completely secure. If a data breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires. Where required, we notify the competent authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.

13. Deletion requests

  • Users and organizations: remove leads from campaigns or delete them in the web application. To close an account or delete an organization's data, contact us (section 19).
  • Prospects: to have your data deleted from a customer's lead list, contact that customer directly, or contact us and we will pass the request on or handle it as the law requires.
  • Extension: removing the extension from Chrome deletes the data it stores in the browser.

We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity first. Deleted data is removed from backups within 30 days.

14. Your rights

  • correct inaccurate data;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests and direct marketing;

To use these rights, contact us (section 19). If we process your data for a customer, we will forward your request to that customer and help them respond.

15. Information for people in our customers' lead lists

If you received a LinkedIn message from one of our customers, your public professional information (name, title, company, location, headline and profile URL) was added to that customer's lead list from LinkedIn search results. This was done by a user of that organization. The customer may have used AI to judge how well your profile fits its campaign, and may store your replies to its messages.

You can object to this processing or ask for deletion at any time. Reply to the sender with a request not to be contacted again, contact the sender's organization, or contact us (section 19).

16. Cookies and local storage

  • Web application: uses the browser's local storage for the sign-in session and small preferences (for example the last selected campaign). It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Browser extension: uses Chrome extension storage for the sign-in session, the selected campaign, imported leads that have not been sent yet, outreach state and short diagnostic logs.
  • This page: hosted on GitHub Pages and sets no cookies of its own.
